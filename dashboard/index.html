How the board is built

Every row is one protocol from the sealed snapshot of , and every number on the board comes out of that file. Nothing here is a list of protocol names written by hand.

implied value = launch odds × ( TVL × capture rate )

1. The curve is fitted on the snapshot's own tokens

The protocols of the snapshot that already carry a token state a market cap against their TVL. Five candidate models predict that ratio from the snapshot's own figures; each one is scored by leave-one-out error, the median percentage it missed a comparable's ratio by when it was predicted from the others. The winner is the model the board runs.

2. Launch odds come from the issuance record

The odds of a token are the share of protocols in the same category that already carry one, smoothed towards the global share when the category is small, then tilted by the cohort of protocols that have been tracked for as long as this one has.

3. The plan under each row is costed

Capital, gas and days come from the protocol's own size and age; the return comes from the same implied value, taken pro-rata over the share of the protocol's book the position represents.

A board's implied value is its own claim, not make.fun's, and it is not a prediction that a token will be issued or when. Protocols whose token already trades are kept out of the board and shown separately, with the market's own numbers.
