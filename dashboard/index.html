How the board is built

Every row is one protocol from the sealed snapshot. The board asks one question of each: if this protocol issued its token tomorrow, what would that token be worth on the record it has today?

implied value = TVL × TVL value × capture rate × launch odds × chain drag

What each term means

  • TVL value is what a dollar of locked value is worth to a token holder once the protocol keeps its cut. It is the same for every row.
  • Capture rate is the share of that value a category can ever convert: a derivatives venue keeps a fat fee on turnover, a bridge keeps almost none. It comes from the category the snapshot records, never from the protocol's name.
  • Launch odds is how likely the token is at all. It is a bell in the protocol's age: a new protocol has not proved itself, one a few years old has, and one a decade old has had its chance. Seven-day growth lifts it, because a protocol that is growing now is one the market is watching.
  • Chain drag is what a wide footprint costs: a token has to launch on every chain at once, and each extra chain makes that harder.

A board's implied value is its own claim, not make.fun's, and it is not a prediction that a token will be issued or when. Protocols whose token already trades are kept out of the board and shown separately, with the market's own numbers.
